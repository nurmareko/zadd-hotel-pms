# ZADD Hotel PMS Visual Language

## Overall Aesthetic

Modern SaaS

Soft Enterprise

Hospitality First

Clean and Operational

---

## Visual Keywords

- soft
- premium
- clean
- structured
- elegant
- lightweight
- professional

---

## Surfaces

Use layered surfaces.

Cards should feel elevated.

Cards should never feel heavy.

Preferred:

- soft shadows
- subtle borders
- bright surfaces

Avoid:

- dark containers
- thick borders
- heavy shadows

---

## Corner Radius

Small Components:
8px

Buttons:
12px

Inputs:
12px

Cards:
16px

Modals:
20px

Maximum:
24px

---

## Color Strategy

Color communicates status.

Color should never be decorative.

Use color intentionally.

---

## Operational Colors

Green

Meaning:

- complete
- ready
- clean
- success

---

Blue

Meaning:

- active
- occupied
- information

---

Amber

Meaning:

- waiting
- dirty
- pending

---

Red

Meaning:

- urgent
- issue
- out of order

---

Purple

Meaning:

- inspection
- special process

---

Gray

Meaning:

- inactive
- unavailable
- archived

---

## Typography

Font Family

Inter

Tone

- neutral
- professional
- invisible

Typography should disappear.

Content should stand out.

---

## Icons

Use simple line icons.

Icons support understanding.

Icons do not become decoration.

Avoid icon overload.

---

## Shadows

Very soft.

Subtle.

Professional.

Never dramatic.

Never floating excessively.

The interface should feel grounded.

---

## White Space

Generous between sections.

Compact within related information.

The interface should breathe without wasting space.

---

## Tables

Tables are tools.

Not reports.

Use:

- soft separators
- minimal borders
- comfortable row heights

Avoid:

- spreadsheet aesthetics
- dense grids
- dark table headers
